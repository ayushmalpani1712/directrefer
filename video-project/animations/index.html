Scene 8: Who Should Choose What? (6:10-7:30)

Which One Is Right for You?

✓ Index Funds

  • ✓ Low cost (0.1-0.5% expense ratio)
  • ✓ Simple to understand
  • ✓ Market returns guaranteed
  • ✓ No manager dependency
  • ✓ Ideal for beginners

★ Active Funds

  • ★ Potential to beat market
  • ★ Professional management
  • ★ Strategic stock picking
  • ★ Higher risk/reward
  • ★ Requires research
Core: Index Funds | Satellite: Selected Active Funds (Optional)
